Privacy Policy

Nookabye is a story-reading app for children and families, provided by RoohAI Services. This policy explains the information used to deliver books, remember preferences and protect subscription access.

Information we do not ask children to provide

Nookabye does not require a child account or ask for a child’s name, exact birthday, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not sell personal information. The app has no advertising, behavioural advertising or social messaging features.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the reading age group selected by a grown-up, preferred story language, narration and music settings, reading progress, saved-book information and optional reminder settings on the device. Downloaded books are stored on the device for offline reading. Secure storage holds installation keys and verified access permissions.

The selected age group is a reading preference, not a request for a child’s exact age or birthday. Reading progress is not uploaded to our story service or sent to RevenueCat by the current app.

Catalogue browsing and book delivery

Unlike a completely bundled story app, Nookabye uses an internet connection to fetch the catalogue, categories, supported languages, cover previews and requested books. Catalogue requests can include the selected reading-age range, language, category and search words. Book-access requests include the story, edition and language requested.

Our services use a randomly generated installation identifier, a public installation key, platform information, integrity proofs and request timestamps to authenticate a device and deliver protected books. These identifiers do not contain a child’s name. A persistent identifier can still distinguish one installation from another, so we treat it as pseudonymous information rather than claiming it is completely anonymous.

Amazon Web Services hosts the story backend and media delivery. Network services process information such as an IP address, request time and technical request details to deliver content, operate the service and investigate failures or abuse.

Subscriptions and purchase verification

Where subscriptions are available, a grown-up can choose an annual subscription for paid stories. Apple or Google processes payment; Nookabye does not receive payment-card details.

RevenueCat is used to verify and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous app customer identifier and purchase, subscription, app, platform and device information needed for this service. Our backend stores the link to the installation, subscription status, verification and expiry dates, and relevant renewal, refund or revocation information. Store subscription events are routed to RevenueCat and our backend to keep access current.

We do not send a child’s name, exact birthday, reading progress or selected reading-age preference to RevenueCat. Purchase information may be retained by the stores and RevenueCat according to their policies and legal obligations. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

Device and app security

Nookabye uses Apple App Attest, Google Play Integrity and Talsec freeRASP to check app authenticity and detect threats such as tampering or an insecure runtime. These checks involve technical app, device, integrity and security-diagnostic information. Talsec’s security service may receive device and app information and detected threat events to provide protection and security reports. These services are used for security, not advertising.

Grown-up settings and purchases use the phone’s supported authentication methods, such as its passcode or biometric unlock. Authentication happens through the operating system. Nookabye does not receive or store the passcode, face image or fingerprint template.

Permissions and reading reminders

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access. Story narration is delivered as recorded audio; the app does not record a child’s voice.

The installed app asks for notification permission for optional reading reminders. If allowed, reminders are scheduled locally on the device. A grown-up can change the time or turn them off in settings. Reminder settings and reading progress are not sent to our servers to schedule these reminders. Declining permission leaves reminders off and does not stop reading.

Offline books and access checks

Saved books and reading progress remain on the device. Paid books require verified subscription access. Offline paid access lasts up to seven days after a successful online verification and never beyond the verified subscription expiry. Reconnecting lets the app refresh story policies and subscription status. These checks use installation, story and subscription information; they do not upload the text a child has read or their reading progress.

Sharing and international processing

We use AWS for hosting and delivery, RevenueCat for subscription verification, Apple and Google for purchases and integrity checks, and Talsec for app protection. Providers process the information needed for those services under their own applicable policies. Our story backend currently operates in the United States, and service providers may process information in other countries.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the security and rights of families and the service. We do not use children’s reading activity for advertising.

Retention, deletion and your choices

You can change reading preferences and reminder settings, remove downloaded books through the app, and remove local app data through your device’s app-storage controls or by uninstalling, subject to the device’s backup and restore settings.

Removing the app does not automatically cancel a subscription or delete server-side installation and purchase records. Manage or cancel a subscription through the store that billed you. Our backend retains installation and subscription records as needed to operate secure delivery, verify access, support restoration and handle refunds or abuse. Standard backend application logs are configured with a 30-day retention period; other records and provider records may have different retention periods.

For a privacy, access or deletion request, email roohailabs@gmail.com. We may need information to locate the relevant installation or purchase and verify the request. Please do not send a child’s name or other unnecessary sensitive information. Some records may need to be retained for legal, transaction or security purposes.

Children’s privacy

Grown-ups manage age preferences, downloads and purchases. We design the app to minimise information requested from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

This website and policy updates

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The latest version and its updated date will appear here.
